Auto Fit Modes
Choose exactly how aggressively the image should fill the frame.
1 — FILL OPENING
No empty space inside the frame. Enlarges image until the opening is completely covered.
2 — MAXIMUM ZOOM
Extremely zoomed in. Great when you want the character filling almost everything.
3 — COVER
Classic cover behavior. Entire frame opening is filled.
4 — CONTAIN
Shows the entire image without cropping.
5 — SMART
Fills most of the opening while keeping more of the character visible.
Recommended: Start with 1 — FILL OPENING, then use the zoom slider if you want the character even closer.
